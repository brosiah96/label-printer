Label Printer privacy

Label Printer processes the label you choose on your iPad and sends it to the configured printer over your local network. The app has no account service, advertising, tracking, analytics service or cloud print server. The developer does not receive your print documents through the app.

Print content

When you use the system Print menu, iPadOS provides the selected print data to the app's printing extension. The extension temporarily stores and converts that data on the iPad, then sends the resulting label to the local Epson printer. It does not upload the document to the developer or a cloud rendering service.

The extension removes its temporary document and image files when processing ends. If iPadOS stops the extension before cleanup completes, the extension removes its leftover temporary files when it next starts. Temporary print documents are limited to 10 MiB per request. This does not control files kept by Google Slides, iPadOS Print Center, or the printer itself.

Local safety information

To track job status and reduce accidental duplicate printing, the extension retains a small history on the iPad. Records include a job number, time, status, an error or recovery message, byte counts, hashes of the print data and request, and whether an interrupted delivery has been reviewed. Hashes are digital fingerprints used to compare jobs; the history does not contain a readable copy of the label, its document title, a user name, or a network password.

The history normally retains up to 100 ordinary recent records, together with active jobs, unresolved deliveries and recent retry records that still need protection. It is bounded by 500 records and 512 KiB. Older ordinary records are pruned as the history is maintained. An unresolved delivery can remain until you review it; there is no fixed time after which it is silently forgotten.

A random installation-specific value keeps the local printer destination stable and limits access to its local endpoint. This value is not an advertising identifier or an account credential. The safety history and destination value are marked to be excluded from device backups. They are not transmitted to the developer.

Permissions and diagnostics

Local Network access lets the app check the configured printer and send your selected label to it. You can change that permission in iPad Settings. Disabling it prevents the app from reaching the printer.

The app writes limited diagnostic events to iPadOS, such as processing stages, job numbers, byte counts and errors. It does not include label artwork or local access values in those messages, and it has no service that uploads these logs to the developer. Apple's device diagnostics and the app you print from are governed by their own settings and policies.

Your choices

You choose when to print. Reviewing an interrupted job records your acknowledgment and does not send another label. You can stop using the app or revoke Local Network permission at any time.

Deleting the app removes its local app data, including its printing history and destination value. Offloading the app may preserve data. Cancel pending jobs and inspect the printer before removing or reinstalling the app, because removing the safety history also removes its record of uncertain deliveries. Reinstallation can create a new printer entry that you need to select again.

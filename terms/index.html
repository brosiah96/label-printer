3. Supported printing and your responsibilities

This release works with the configured Epson ColorWorks CW-C6000Pu on the local network, using 108 x 152.4 mm media, one selected page and one copy per request. It is not a universal printer driver. It requires compatible iPad software, Local Network permission, a reachable configured printer, and suitable supplies. Other devices, printers, paper sizes, apps, and future operating-system versions may behave differently.

You choose when and what to print. Check the source document, selected printer, page, quantity, label size, and physical result before using the output. Network delivery or a completed job status does not independently establish that a correct physical label printed. After an interrupted job, inspect the printer and cancel any pending system job before clearing a hold or printing again. Clearing a hold acknowledges your review; it does not print a replacement.

Keep source documents separately. The App is not a document archive. Maintain an alternative process for time-sensitive work. The App does not verify prices, ingredients, allergens, barcodes, legal notices, or other label content and should not be the sole safeguard for safety-critical or legally required labeling. You are responsible for checking that your labels satisfy the requirements applicable to their use. These responsibilities do not waive rights that the law does not allow you to waive.
